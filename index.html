00 · Why Klaio

The payout math rarely explains itself.Klaio shows you why, then writes the fix back to your books.

Shopify pays out one lump sum. Xero and QuickBooks only ever see that number, never what it's made of. Merchants have been asking why their sales and their bank balance don't match for years, on Shopify's own forums and in reviews of the tools meant to fix it. Most of those tools are USD-first and Xero-only, matching totals without checking whether the tax code behind them was right. Klaio does both.

Sales and payouts rarely match

Neither Shopify nor your accounting software tells you why. Klaio reads the full payout breakdown and shows exactly what happened to every pound.

Matching isn't the same as correct

Tools that promise "perfect reconciliation" usually just confirm the numbers add up. They don't check whether the VAT code behind them was right, and sellers have had to catch that gap on their own.

Most tools assume you're already caught up

A 60-day nightly sync only works if you were reconciled to begin with. Fall behind, and you need a tool built to catch you up, not one built around staying current.

01 · Built for UK VAT

Categorisation that knows yourVAT scheme, not just your chart of accounts.

Move onto the Flat Rate Scheme and your margin shifts overnight. Issue a partial refund and Shopify may not reverse the VAT the way you'd expect; merchants have flagged this on Shopify's own forums for years with no fix. Sell into more than one jurisdiction and your tax-code mapping turns into a spreadsheet nobody wants to own. Most reconciliation tools are built USD-first and treat VAT as an afterthought. Klaio is priced in GBP, thinks in UK VAT schemes, and was built for HMRC from day one instead of retrofitted for it.

Flat-rate scheme aware

The moment you move onto the VAT Flat Rate Scheme, your margin shifts and every payout fee needs recoding to match it. A tool built for standard-rate sellers won't do that automatically. Klaio knows your scheme and categorises accordingly.

Partial exemption handling

If part of your business falls under partial exemption rules, Klaio flags transactions that need splitting rather than silently lumping them into one recoverable-VAT bucket. That kind of blanket categorisation looks fine until your accountant catches it at year-end.

Correct VAT on refunds

Partial refunds are a known blind spot. Shopify can process them as VAT-free credits, understating tax and overstating net sales in your reports. Klaio checks the original transaction's VAT treatment before applying the reversal, rather than mirroring the refund amount.

03 · Validation, not just matching

We don't just copy your tax codes.We check whether they're right.

Tools that market "perfect reconciliation" are usually only checking that the numbers add up, not that the tax code was right to begin with. Sellers using these tools have flagged the same wrong default tax-code mappings for years, only to be told to check with their accountant. Klaio's AI reasons about the transaction itself and surfaces cases where the applied VAT treatment looks inconsistent with how similar items are usually coded.

It's a deliberately narrow claim: Klaio flags for review; it never decides VAT treatment on your behalf. The judgment call always stays with you or your accountant. But knowing what to look at is worth a lot more than a tool that just repeats what's already there.

04 · Built to scale across clients & gateways

One view across every client.Every gateway, beyond Xero and Shopify Payments.

Single-integration tools work fine until a client also pays through Stripe, and the two gateways start building up separate clearing balances that never quite zero out. Or a channel gets connected twice (once as a direct integration, once as a Shopify payment gateway), and revenue gets counted twice until someone catches it. Or their books live in QuickBooks instead of Xero. Or you manage twelve clients instead of one, each on a different combination of tools. Klaio was built for the portfolio view from the start.

  • Xero and QuickBooks: not locked to a single ledger.
  • Shopify Payments, Stripe, PayPal, Klarna: reconciled in one place, not stitched together from clearing accounts that never quite match.
